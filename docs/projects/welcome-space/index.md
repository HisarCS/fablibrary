# Welcome Space – Mission 01

!!! warning "Draft v1: nothing has been physically tested yet"
    All dimensions are estimates. Check the Assumptions page and the First week plan before cutting or printing.

The first project where grade 4 students meet the FabLab. In 60 minutes, every student builds an LED-lit spaceship they personalize themselves and take home.

**Digital Design → Digital Fabrication → Electronics → Assembly → Testing → Functional Product**

![The finished v1 ship, by day and in a dark room](img/v1_day_night_illustration.svg)

## Make it yours

Every student gets one sticker sheet (printed and cut on the Roland BN-20): 3 window stickers, 3 planets, 5 stars, 3 wing patterns, 2 stripes, a blank name label and a Bug Hunter badge. The pink line is the cut contour.

## Design principles

- Electronics in **one place**: under the base plate, on a single face, tested before mechanical assembly.
- Glue-free press-fit, solderless circuit, few parts.
- Poka-yoke: panel slot 28 mm, wing slot 20 mm; the wrong part does not fit the wrong slot.
- Mistakes are instructive, not destructive.

## Scale

6 classes, ~140 students, 160 kits (spares included). Details: [kit list](kit-list.md). Before building with children, read the [safety notes](safety.md).
