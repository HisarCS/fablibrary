# Assembly

![Assembly sequence](img/v1_assembly_drawing.svg)

1. Stickers: on panels and wings while they are flat
2. Electronics: plate upside down (bottom face up) – holder, 2 copper rails, 3 LEDs
3. Test: insert battery, ON. If not lit, follow the 5-step troubleshooting
4. Panels ×3: into the long slots on the top face of the plate
5. Plate into diffuser: electronics facing down
6. Nose cone: onto the top tabs of the 3 panels
7. Wings ×3: into the short slots
8. Dark room: everyone switches ON together

!!! warning "To be tested in the pilot"
    The order of steps 4-5-6. Alternative: panels into the nose cone first, then everything into the plate.
