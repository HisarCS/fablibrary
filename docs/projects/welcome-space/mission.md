# Mission: How was this spaceship made?

:material-rocket-launch: Your ship was made by four different machines working together!

| Part | Machine | What did it do? |
|---|---|---|
| Body, wings, base | Laser cutter (Epilog, xTool) | Cut the wood with light and drew lines on it |
| Nose cone and exhaust | 3D printer (Bambu Lab) | Melted plastic layer by layer into shape |
| Copper rails | Roland GS-24 | Cut the copper tape with a blade |
| Stickers | Roland BN-20 | Printed and cut them |
| Light | You! | You built the circuit and lit the LEDs |

## Did you know?
The **long leg of an LED goes to plus (+)**, the short leg to minus (−). If you put it in backwards, it won't light, but it won't break either. Find it and fix it, and you become a **Bug Hunter**!
