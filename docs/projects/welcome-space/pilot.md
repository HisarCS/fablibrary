# Pilot lesson: observation form

Run the pilot with **6–8 students** in one 60-minute session, with two adults observing. Print this page or fill it in on a laptop. Do **not** write student names: use the pilot code printed on the base plate or a number.

Gate for the lesson: at least **80% of students** finish a working ship within 60 minutes.

## 5. The ship

- Does it stand upright on the table, without wobbling?
- Can a child change the cell and reach the switch easily?
- Dark room: are all three LEDs clearly visible from 3 m?
- Is the nose cone visibly lit? (assumption A10)
- Photos taken (no student faces): yes / no

## 6. Student survey (3 questions, 1 minute)

Optional: *"What would you change on your ship?"*

## 7. Adult debrief (10 minutes after the lesson)

1. Which step cost the most time?
2. Which instruction did students not understand?
3. Which tolerance or part should change before the next class?
4. Was the adult-to-student ratio enough? Where did you wish for a fourth adult?
5. Gate: did 80% finish within 60 minutes?
